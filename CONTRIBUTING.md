# Contributing

Thank you for taking the time.

## Where does my contribution go?

| You have … | Use |
|---|---|
| an idea, critique or experience with a pilot question | Discussions → category **Ideas** |
| a technical question about an example | Discussions → category **Q&A** |
| a reproducible defect in an example | Issues → **Bug report** |
| a fix for an example | Pull request referencing the Issue or Discussion |

Experience from everyday work without a technical angle fits the CUYOCO Community (German):
https://community.cuyoco.com. Please do not post the same thing in both places.

## What happens with feedback

1. We read every Discussion and Issue in a weekly review.
2. A person, not a bot, decides whether a contribution is a question, a problem, an improvement or out of scope.
3. Reviewed suggestions are passed to the responsible CUYOCO area as a proposal. Nothing posted here becomes a
   CUYOCO statement or product decision automatically.
4. We post a short note on what we adopted, tested, rejected or left open.

## Data and conduct

- No personal data, customer data, credentials or confidential details from your organisation.
- We store a link to your post and a short excerpt for the review, never your profile data.
- Be specific and kind. Moderators may hide off-topic or abusive posts.

## Code contributions

- Examples stay dependency-free (Python standard library) and use synthetic data only.
- Add or update the tests next to the example; `python3 test_*.py` must pass.
- By opening a pull request you agree that your contribution is published under the licence of that directory.
